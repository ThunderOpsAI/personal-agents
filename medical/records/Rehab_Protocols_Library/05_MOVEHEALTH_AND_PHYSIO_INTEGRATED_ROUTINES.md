## 1. Movehealth+ Program 1: Shoulder & Upper Back Resistance Band Routine
*Target: Scapular stabilization, rotator cuff recruitment, mid-back strengthening while protecting the C7–T1 junction and frozen shoulder.*

| Exercise | Equipment | Prescribed Prescription | Form Cues & Biomechanical Modifications |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Low Row with Exercise Band** | Exercise Band | 3 sets · 10 reps | Anchor band at waist level. Pull elbows back close to rib cage. Squeeze shoulder blades down and back. Do not shrug. |
| **Upright Row with Exercise Band** | Exercise Band | 3 sets · 10 reps | *Modification*: Pull hands only to lower chest height (elbows stay strictly below shoulder level) to prevent subacromial impingement. |
| **W Row with Exercise Band** | Exercise Band | 3 sets · 10 reps | Anchor band at chest level. Pull back flaring elbows to 45°, forming a 'W' shape. Squeeze lower trapezius and rhomboids. |
| **Shoulder External Rotation with Band** | Exercise Band | 3 sets · 10 reps | Place a rolled towel under the elbow. Keep elbow pinned to side at 90°. Rotate forearm outward smoothly. |
| **Shoulder Internal Rotation with Band** | Exercise Band | 3 sets · 10 reps | Towel under elbow. Rotate forearm inward across belly against band tension. |
| **Scapular Stabilization with Shoulder Flexion** | Light Band / Bodyweight | 3 sets · 10 reps | Raise arm forward only up to 70°–80° (stop before shoulder level). Focus on serratus anterior protraction. |
| **Shoulder Flexion with Exercise Band** | Exercise Band | 3 sets · 10 reps | Stand on band. Raise arm forward with thumb pointed up. Stop at 80° elevation. Do not arch lower back. |
| **Shrugs at 30 Degrees with Dumbbells** | Dumbbells (Light 1–2 kg) | 3 sets · 10 reps | Lean torso forward 30° with neutral spine. Elevate and retract scapulae diagonally. Release slowly. |

## 2. Movehealth+ Program 2: Neck & Upper Back Mobility Routine
*Target: Active cervical range of motion and thoracic mobility without stress on C4–C7 surgical fusion.*

| Exercise | Starting Position | Prescribed Prescription | Form Cues & Safety Cues |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Open Book** | Side-lying, knees bent | 3 sets · 10 reps | Clamp a pillow firmly between knees. Sweep top arm open across chest. Rotate only through mid-back; neck stays aligned with chest. |
| **Thread the Needle** | Quadruped / Seated | 3 sets · 10 reps | Slide hand through under opposing armpit. Move only through thoracic spine; avoid resting head or weight onto neck. |
| **AROM Cervical Rotation in Supine** | Supine, head supported | 3 sets · 10 reps | Lie flat on mat. Slowly turn head to right within comfortable range, return to center, then turn left. Smooth motion, zero jerking. |
| **Roll Down Against the Wall** | Standing back to wall | 3 sets · 10 reps | *Modification*: Perform gentle upper thoracic roll forward only. Keep lumbar spine neutral against wall; do not bend forward at waist. |
| **Deep Neck Flexors in Supine** | Supine flat | 3 sets · 10 reps | Perform subtle chin tuck (nodding motion making double chin). Hold 5 seconds while flattening back of neck toward mat. |

## 3. Movehealth+ Program 3: Motor Control in Four-Point Kneeling
*Target: Deep core stabilization, scapular protraction, and glute activation. Use thick padding under knees.*

| Exercise | Position | Prescribed Prescription | Form Cues & Clinical Rules |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Upper Cervical Retraction in Four-Point Kneeling** | Quadruped (Hands & Knees) | 3 sets · 10 reps | Keep spine horizontal. Draw chin backward away from floor without tilting head up or down. |
| **Knee Hovers in Four-Point Kneeling** | Quadruped, toes tucked | 3 sets · 30s hold | Tuck toes, brace core, lift knees 1 inch off mat. Keep spine flat like a tabletop. |
| **Pelvic Tilt in Four-Point Kneeling** | Quadruped | 3 sets · 10 reps | Isolate pelvis. Slowly tuck tailbone under, then return to neutral. Avoid excessive sway back. |
| **Scapula Protraction in Four-Point Kneeling** | Quadruped | 3 sets · 10 reps | Press hands into mat to push upper back toward ceiling (spreading shoulder blades). Lower chest with control. |
| **Isometric Deep Neck Flexors in Four-Point Kneeling**| Quadruped | 3 sets · 30s hold | Hold chin retracted in neutral alignment against gravity for 30 seconds. Do not let head drop forward. |
| **Four-Point Kneeling Bent Knee Donkey Kicks** | Quadruped | 3 sets · 10 reps | Drive heel toward ceiling keeping knee bent at 90°. Engage glute without arching lumbar spine. |
| **Pregnancy / Modified Arm Raises in Quadruped** | Quadruped | 3 sets · 10 reps | Lift one arm forward below shoulder level while maintaining strict trunk stillness. Alternating arms. |

---
*Safety Note: If kneeling causes discomfort to the left knee post-osteotomy, perform the quadruped exercises standing with hands resting on a sturdy table or bench.*
