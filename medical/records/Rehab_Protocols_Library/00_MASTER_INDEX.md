## 2. Core Protocol Overview

### Section 02: Hydrotherapy Protocols (30–90 Minutes)
*Heavy resistance bands, water buoyancy, and rail anchoring to bypass gravity while maximizing load:*
- **H-MED1 (30 min)**: Dynamic Aqua Balance & Banded Core (Medium)
- **H-MED2 (45 min)**: Total Body Aquatic Conditioning & Scapular Rowing (Medium)
- **H-EXP1 (60 min)**: Heavy Hydro Resistance & Power Protocol (Expert)
- **H-EXP2 (75 min)**: Multi-Planar Hydro Endurance & Kinetic Chain Overhaul (Expert)
- **H-EXP3 (90 min)**: Master Hydro Athlete Rehabilitation Protocol (Expert)
- *Classic Aqua Series*: A1 Water Reset (~6 min), A2 Side-Step (~6 min), A3 Balanced Aqua (~14 min), A4 Low-Impact Aqua (~14 min), A5 Full Aqua (~25 min), A6 Aqua Variety (~25 min).

### Section 03: Floor-Based Yoga Routines (20–60 Minutes)
*Preserves cervical neutrality (C4–C7) and unloads the left knee and right shoulder:*
- **Y-MED1 (20–25 min)**: Posture, Scapula & Core Alignment (Wall angels partial range, Dead Bug arms only, glute bridges)
- **Y-MED2 (30 min)**: Spine & Pelvic Stability Flow (Open Book thoracic sweep, supported bridge holds)
- **Y-MED3 (40 min)**: Restorative Decompression & Nerve Gliding (Median/ulnar sliders, supine cervical AROM)
- **Y-EXP1 (45 min)**: Total Body Isometric Yoga & Pilates Hybrid (Resistance bands on feet, Pilates bar leg press)
- **Y-EXP2 (60 min)**: Comprehensive Spinal Rehabilitation & Kinetic Strength (Deep neck flexors, scapular W-raises, banded clams)
- *Land Routines 28–30*: Routine 28 (Posture + Core, 20–25m), Routine 29 (Recovery Session, 20–30m), Routine 30 (Mixed Variety, 25–30m).

### Section 04: Beginner Mindful Flows (10–30 Minutes)
*Low intensity, zero knee impact, suitable for high flare or fatigue days:*
- **Yoga Flows**:
  - `Y-BEG1 (10 min)`: Morning Gentle Reset (Constructive rest, pelvic tilts, heel slides)
  - `Y-BEG2 (20 min)`: Restorative Hip & Lumbar Unwind (Supported Child's Pose over bolster, single knee-to-chest)
  - `Y-BEG3 (30 min)`: Full-Body Decompression & Nervous System Downregulation
- **Tai Chi Protocols**:
  - `TC-BEG1 (10 min)`: Seated Mindful Energy & Breath Flow (Commencing Form, Seated Cloud Hands, Closing)
  - `TC-BEG2 (20 min)`: Supported Standing Balance & Kinetic Grounding (Weight shifts holding rail, Repulse the Monkey low reach, Zhan Zhuang)
  - `TC-BEG3 (30 min)`: Restorative Flow for Chronic Pain & Vitality (Six Harmonies, Tai Chi walking along rail, Parting Wild Horse's Mane)

### Section 05: Movehealth+ Integrated Protocols
*Direct mapping of prescribed clinic exercises:*
- Shoulder & Upper Back Band Complex: Low Row, Upright Row (<80°), W-Row, External/Internal Rotations, Scapular Stabilization with Flexion, 30° Shrugs.
- Four-Point Kneeling Motor Control: Upper cervical retraction, Knee hovers, Pelvic tilt, Scapular protraction, Isometric deep neck flexors, Bent-knee donkey kicks.
- Neck & Upper Back Stretching: Open book, Thread the needle, Supine AROM cervical rotation, Wall roll-downs, Supine deep neck flexors.
