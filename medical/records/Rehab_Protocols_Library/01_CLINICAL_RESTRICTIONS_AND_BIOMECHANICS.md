# Clinical Restrictions & Biomechanical Safety Rules

**Document**: `01_CLINICAL_RESTRICTIONS_AND_BIOMECHANICS.md`  
**Governing Roles**: Surgeon Persona (`SURGEON`) & Physiotherapist Persona (`PHYSIO`)  
**Context**: C4–C7 Anterior/Posterior Cervical Fusion, C7–T1 Junction Radiculopathy, Left Shoulder Mobility Restriction, Left Knee Post-Osteotomy, Right Ankle Instability.

---

## 1. Absolute Clinical Invariants & Red Lines

1. **Cervical Spine Protection (C4–C7 Fusion & C7–T1 Junction)**:
   - **Neutral Alignment Mandate**: The cervical spine must remain strictly neutral during all exercises.
   - **No Forced End-Range Motion**: Prohibit forced cervical hyperextension, aggressive rotation, or high-velocity manipulation.
   - **Contraindicated Movements**: Classical Pilates Hundred with head/shoulder curl is prohibited. Head must remain supported flat or on a contoured thin pad.
   - **Foam Rolling Prohibition**: Foam rolling of the neck and lower lumbar spine is strictly prohibited.

2. **Right Shoulder & Thoracic Outlet Protection (Frozen Shoulder / C7–T1 Nerve Compression)**:
   - **Elevation Boundary**: Active abduction or flexion under load must stay below 80°–90°.
   - **Rowing Biomechanics**: All resistance band rows (Low row, W-row, Upright row) must keep elbows tucked down to prevent subacromial impingement and burning nerve flare-ups.
   - **Submerged Hydro Dynamics**: Arm sweeps and resistance presses in water must be performed below shoulder depth.

3. **Lower Kinetic Chain (Left Knee Post-Osteotomy & Right Ankle Instability)**:
   - **Bracing Protocol**: Left knee brace and right ankle brace must be worn during all standing and loaded floor exercises.
   - **Joint Loading Limits**: No deep squats, deep lunges, crouching, or kneeling on hard unpadded surfaces. 
   - **Four-Point Kneeling Modification**: Must use thick cushioned matting under knees or substitute with quadruped standing hands-on-bench or supine equivalents.

4. **Pain Scoring Adaptation**:
   - If pre-exercise pain score is $\ge 7/10$, pause active loaded strengthening. Transition immediately to warm water hydrotherapy, low-intensity decompression, or passive restorative rest.
   - Sharp, shooting, or electric burning spikes (especially C7–T1 junction) indicate immediate exercise cessation and application of cryotherapy.

---

## 2. Aquatic Biomechanics & Band Integration Rationale

- **Buoyancy Unloading**: In chest-deep water, body weight is reduced by 60%–75%, eliminating axial spinal compression and patellofemoral shear force on the left knee.
- **Hydrostatic Pressure**: External fluid pressure dampens nociceptive pain signaling, promotes venous and lymphatic return, and reduces localized joint edema.
- **Fluid Resistance & Bands**: Fluid drag provides multi-planar concentric resistance without aggressive eccentric muscle damage. Combining heavy Therabands anchored to pool rails provides intense muscular overload while the buoyant water safely cushions and stabilizes the joints.
