# Hydrotherapy Resistance Protocols (30–90 Minutes)

**Document**: `02_HYDROTHERAPY_PROTOCOLS_30_TO_90_MIN.md`  
**Governing Roles**: Physiotherapist (`PHYSIO`) & Medical Researcher (`RESEARCHER`)  
**Target Environment**: Warm Hydrotherapy Pool (30°C–34°C / 86°F–93°F)  
**Target Equipment**: Heavy & Medium Therabands, Heavy Loop Ankle Bands, Pool Rail, Flotation Belt, Kickboard, Hydro Dumbbells, Pool Noodles.

---

## 1. Medium Intensity Protocols (30–45 Minutes)

### H-MED1: 30-Minute Dynamic Aqua Balance & Banded Core
- **Duration**: 30 Minutes
- **Intensity**: Medium
- **Equipment**: Medium Theraband, Pool Rail, Flotation Noodle
- **Focus**: Kinetic chain gait stability, core engagement, pelvic neutral positioning.
- **Protocol**:
  1. **Dynamic Aquatic Warm-Up (5 mins)**:
     - Forward walking (heel-to-toe) in chest-deep water (2 mins).
     - Backward walking (toe-to-heel) engaging hamstrings/glutes (2 mins).
     - Lateral side-stepping holding pool rail (1 min).
  2. **Banded Rail-Anchored Hip Abduction & Extension (8 mins)**:
     - Anchor medium band to pool rail; loop around ankle.
     - Stand tall, holding rail. Sweep leg out laterally against band tension and water drag. 3 sets x 12 reps per leg.
     - Sweep leg straight back into hip extension (torso upright, no lumbar arching). 3 sets x 12 reps per leg.
  3. **Pool Wall Pelvic Neutral & Core Wall Press (7 mins)**:
     - Back flat against pool wall. Engage deep transversus abdominis; flatten lower spine into wall. Hold 10s x 6 reps.
     - Hold flotation noodle submerged at chest level. Press downward, engaging lats and core without flexing the neck. 3 sets x 10 reps.
  4. **Noodle-Supported Deep Water Core Suspensions (6 mins)**:
     - Noodle under armpits in chest/deep water. Alternate slow knee lifts toward chest against water resistance. 3 sets x 15 reps.
  5. **Buoyancy Decompression Cool-Down (4 mins)**:
     - Supported supine water float with noodle under knees and neck support. Deep diaphragmatic breathing.

---

### H-MED2: 45-Minute Total Body Aquatic Conditioning & Scapular Rowing
- **Duration**: 45 Minutes
- **Intensity**: Medium
- **Equipment**: Heavy/Medium Theraband, Pool Rail, Kickboard, Water Dumbbells
- **Focus**: Upper back scapular stabilization, shallow squats without knee shear, multi-planar endurance.
- **Protocol**:
  1. **Hydrodynamic Gait & Scapular Activation (10 mins)**:
     - Forward, backward, and lateral water strides (5 mins).
     - Submerged arm sweeps and aquatic chin retractions (chest submerged) (5 mins).
  2. **Rail-Anchored Submerged Band Rows & W-Rows (10 mins)**:
     - Anchor heavy band to rail at waist height.
     - Step back to create tension. Pull handles back, squeezing shoulder blades together. Keep elbows strictly below shoulder height (no impingement). 3 sets x 12 reps.
     - W-Row: Elbows flared slightly, hands at lower chest level. Squeeze lower traps. 3 sets x 10 reps.
  3. **Banded Pool-Rail Shallow Squat Presses (10 mins)**:
     - Heavy band looped around rail and pelvis.
     - Facing rail, sink into shallow water squat (max 45° knee flexion). Drive through heels and glutes against band pull. 4 sets x 10 reps.
  4. **Kickboard Aquatic Core Presses & Twists (10 mins)**:
     - Submerge kickboard vertically with both hands. Push down into water column. 3 sets x 12 reps.
     - Gentle torso rotations with kickboard submerged (keep cervical spine rigid and eyes forward). 3 sets x 10 reps.
  5. **Decompression & Ankle Flushes (5 mins)**:
     - Hold rail, gentle ankle circles and calf stretches against pool step.

## 2. Expert High-Resistance Protocols (60–90 Minutes)

### H-EXP1: 60-Minute Heavy Hydro Resistance & Power Protocol
- **Duration**: 60 Minutes
- **Intensity**: Expert / High Resistance
- **Equipment**: Heavy Theraband (Black/Silver), Heavy Loop Bands, Pool Rail, Water Dumbbells, Flotation Belt
- **Focus**: High muscular overload without joint impact, power output, core stabilization.
- **Protocol**:
  1. **Dynamic Thermal & Gait Warm-Up (10 mins)**:
     - High-knee water marching (3 mins).
     - Multi-planar lateral walk-outs holding rail (3 mins).
     - Submerged arm pendulum and rotator cuff warm-up sweeps (4 mins).
  2. **Heavy Banded Pool-Rail Power Squat Pulses (10 mins)**:
     - Heavy Theraband around waist anchored to rail.
     - Perform shallow explosive pulses (30°–45° knee flexion), driving up forcefully through glutes against band tension. 4 sets of 12 reps (60s rest).
  3. **Submerged Unilateral Band Rows & Chest Press (10 mins)**:
     - Anchor band to rail. 
     - Left arm: Heavy powerful rows against band tension and water drag. 4 sets x 12 reps.
     - Right arm: Controlled, micro-range pulls to tolerance below 80° elevation. 4 sets x 8 reps.
  4. **Banded Submerged Knee Extensions & Lateral Kicks (12 mins)**:
     - Heavy band around ankle anchored to pool wall.
     - Facing away from rail: extend knee against band and water resistance. 4 sets x 12 reps per leg.
     - Facing side: heavy hip abductions against band. 4 sets x 12 reps per leg.
  5. **Deep Water Suspended Core Crucible (10 mins)**:
     - Flotation belt on, deep water. 
     - Alternate 45-second high-cadence bicycle pedaling with 45-second double knee-to-chest tucks. 5 cycles.
  6. **Thermal Decompression & Fascial Stretching (8 mins)**:
     - Pool wall calf decompressions, hamstring stretches using buoyancy, passive traction hanging from noodle.

### H-EXP2: 75-Minute Multi-Planar Hydro Endurance & Kinetic Chain Overhaul
- **Duration**: 75 Minutes
- **Intensity**: Expert / High Resistance
- **Equipment**: Heavy Loop Bands, Heavy Therabands, Pool Rail, Water Dumbbells, Pool Noodle
- **Focus**: Maximum lactate clearance, endurance, kinetic chain realignment, rotational core stability.
- **Protocol**:
  1. **Hydrodynamic Activation Circuit (12 mins)**:
     - 400m water stride combination (forward walking, backward walking, carioca crossover steps).
  2. **Banded Lateral Walk-Outs & Monster Walks (15 mins)**:
     - Heavy loop band around both ankles in chest-deep water.
     - Side-step across the pool against band tension and water drag. 4 sets x 15 steps left and right.
     - Monster walks (wide zig-zag strides forward and backward). 4 sets x 12 strides.
  3. **Submerged Heavy Dumbbell Upper Body Matrix (13 mins)**:
     - Water dumbbells submerged at mid-chest depth.
     - Bicep curls into submerged low-punches (elbows low). 3 sets x 12 reps.
     - Torso rotations with dumbbells held at ribs (thoracic only, neck locked). 3 sets x 12 reps.
  4. **Rail-Supported Explosive Glute Kickbacks with Heavy Band (12 mins)**:
     - Band anchored to pool rail, looped to ankle.
     - Drive leg straight back into maximum glute contraction. Hold 2s at peak. 4 sets x 12 reps per leg.
  5. **Deep Water Interval Treading & Core Sprints (13 mins)**:
     - Deep water with flotation belt.
     - 1-minute maximum effort water treading / high knee churn.
     - 1-minute slow recovery sculling. Repeat for 6 rounds.
  6. **Buoyancy Elongation & Lymphatic Drainage (10 mins)**:
     - Full supine float with noodles under head and knees. Passive hydrostatic lymphatic flush.

### H-EXP3: 90-Minute Master Hydro Athlete Rehabilitation Protocol
- **Duration**: 90 Minutes
- **Intensity**: Expert / Maximum Resistance
- **Equipment**: Full Hydro Resistance Kit (Heavy bands, Hydro paddles, Flotation belt, Noodles, Rail)
- **Focus**: Complete full-body athletic conditioning bypassing all land gravity limitations.
- **Protocol**:
  1. **Phase 1: Hydrodynamic Multi-Speed Gait & Thermal Prep (15 mins)**:
     - Progressive speed water walking (slow -> moderate -> fast stride).
     - Submerged shoulder sculling and scapular retractions.
  2. **Phase 2: Heavy Band Upper-Body Kinetic Chain (20 mins)**:
     - Heavy rail-anchored Low Rows (3 sets x 12).
     - Heavy W-Rows (3 sets x 10).
     - External/Internal shoulder rotation complexes with band (3 sets x 10 per arm, keeping right arm low).
     - Scapular protraction/retraction holds in chest-deep water (3 sets x 12).
  3. **Phase 3: Lower Kinetic Chain & Pelvic Power (20 mins)**:
     - Heavy banded rail squats with 3-second isometric pause at 45°. 4 sets x 10 reps.
     - Banded submerged knee extensions for quadriceps hypertrophy. 4 sets x 12 reps per leg.
     - Banded lateral walk-outs and rail-supported glute kickbacks. 4 sets x 12 reps per leg.
  4. **Phase 4: Deep Water Aquatic Core Crucible (18 mins)**:
     - Deep water suspended scissor kicks against water resistance. 4 sets x 45 seconds.
     - Submerged kickboard presses (pressing board straight down into water column). 4 sets x 12 reps.
     - Deep water high-knees interval sprints (flotation belt on). 5 rounds of 1 min on / 1 min off.
  5. **Phase 5: Neuromuscular Gait Integration & Balance (10 mins)**:
     - Single-leg balance holds in chest-deep water (holding rail as needed). 2 mins per leg.
     - Heel-to-toe tightrope water walking with contralateral arm sweeps. 6 mins.
  6. **Phase 6: Passive Decompression & Thermal Surrender (7 mins)**:
     - Noodle-supported vertical spinal traction.
     - Supine float with deep diaphragmatic parasympathetic breathwork.

## 3. Short & Classic Aqua Routines (A1–A6 Library)

- **A1. Water Reset (~6 min)**: Forward/backward walking (2 min), knee lifts (1 min), small torso movements (1 min), supported standing recovery (2 min).
- **A2. Side-Step Reset (~6 min)**: Side walking (2 min), rail knee lifts (1 min), supported leg movements (1 min), pool edge recovery (2 min).
- **A3. Balanced Aqua (~14 min)**: Water walking (3 min), supported kicks (2 min), hip abduction holding rail (2 min), arm push/pull below shoulder (2 min), knee lifts (2 min), recovery (3 min).
- **A4. Low-Impact Aqua (~14 min)**: Forward/backward walking (3 min), side walking (2 min), supported leg swings (2 min), arm press elbows low (2 min), knee lifts (2 min), recovery (3 min).
- **A5. Full Aqua Session (~25 min)**: Water walking (5 min), aqua cycling motion (3 min), flutter kicks on wall (3 min), side leg raises (3 min), arm push/pull (3 min), walking (3 min), supported rest (5 min).
- **A6. Aqua Variety (~25 min)**: Forward/backward walking (5 min), side walking (3 min), supported knee lifts (3 min), leg kicks (3 min), arm resistance (3 min), walking (3 min), recovery (5 min).

*Safety Note: Verify pool rail anchor stability before loading heavy resistance bands. Maintain adequate hydration before and after warm pool sessions.*
