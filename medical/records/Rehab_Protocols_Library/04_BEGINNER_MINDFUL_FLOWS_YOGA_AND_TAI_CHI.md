# Beginner Mindful Flows: Yoga & Tai Chi (10–30 Minutes)

**Document**: `04_BEGINNER_MINDFUL_FLOWS_YOGA_AND_TAI_CHI.md`  
**Governing Roles**: Physiotherapist (`PHYSIO`) & Neuro-PPS (`NEURO_PPS`)  
**Target State**: Flare recovery, morning stiffness, high mental fatigue, zero joint impact.  
**Clinical Design**: Zero knee shearing, strictly neutral cervical alignment, no unpadded kneeling, low arm elevations (<80°).

---

## 1. Beginner Yoga Flows (Floor-Based & Supported)

### Flow Y-BEG1: 10-Minute Morning Gentle Reset
- **Duration**: 10 Minutes
- **Intensity**: Very Beginner / Zero Impact
- **Equipment**: Mat, Bolster/Pillow
- **Focus**: Awakening circulation and resetting lumbar curvature before standing.
- **Protocol**:
  1. **Constructive Rest Position (3 mins)**: Lie supine on mat. Bolster under knees to relax hip flexors and decompress lumbar spine. Hands on belly. Deep diaphragmatic breathing.
  2. **Supine Pelvic Tilts (3 mins)**: Inhale, gentle natural arch in lower back. Exhale, gently flatten lumbar spine into the mat. 10 slow smooth repetitions.
  3. **Alternating Heel Slides (2 mins)**: Slowly slide right heel out until leg is straight, then slide back. Repeat left. Keep pelvis completely still.
  4. **Diaphragmatic Centering Breath (2 mins)**: Rest hands on lower ribs. Inhale expand 360°, exhale release jaw and neck tension.

---

### Flow Y-BEG2: 20-Minute Restorative Hip & Lumbar Unwind
- **Duration**: 20 Minutes
- **Intensity**: Very Beginner / Restorative
- **Equipment**: Mat, Bolster, Yoga Strap
- **Focus**: Unloading pelvic tightness, piriformis release, gentle hip mobility.
- **Protocol**:
  1. **Supported Child's Pose over Bolster (5 mins)**: Knees wide on padded mat, place large bolster between thighs. Fold torso forward resting chest and head on bolster. Neck stays in neutral extension.
  2. **Supine Single Knee-to-Chest (5 mins)**: Lie supine. Gently draw right knee toward chest (hug behind hamstring, not over knee cap). Hold 2.5 mins. Repeat left.
  3. **Supine Figure-Four with Strap (5 mins)**: Cross right ankle over left knee. Loop yoga strap around left thigh to assist gentle pull toward chest without straining right shoulder. 2.5 mins per side.
  4. **Supported Savasana (5 mins)**: Rest supine with bolster under knees. Arms resting at sides, palms up.

---

### Flow Y-BEG3: 30-Minute Full-Body Decompression & Downregulation
- **Duration**: 30 Minutes
- **Intensity**: Very Beginner / Restorative
- **Equipment**: Mat, Bolster, Yoga Block, Small Head Pillow
- **Focus**: Autonomic nervous system regulation, vagal tone, cervical relaxation.
- **Protocol**:
  1. **Constructive Rest with Vagal Breath (6 mins)**: Supine, knees supported by bolster. 4s inhale through nose, 7s smooth exhale through lips to stimulate parasympathetic tone.
  2. **Supine AROM Cervical Rotation & Chin Tucks (5 mins)**: Gentle active chin tucks (double chin) without lifting head off pillow (10 reps). Slow active turns of head left and right within comfortable arc (10 reps).
  3. **Open Book Thoracic Mobilization (7 mins)**: Side-lying with pillow between knees. Sweep top arm open across chest, rotating upper back while head rests supported on pillow. 10 slow reps each side.
  4. **Supported Bridge on Lowest Block Height (6 mins)**: Slide yoga block under sacrum. Rest weight entirely into block, feeling passive opening across anterior hips.
  5. **Legs-Elevated Wall Relaxation (6 mins)**: Lie with hips near wall or couch, elevate calves onto couch surface. Rest hands on lower abdomen.

## 2. Beginner Tai Chi Protocols (Seated & Supported Standing)

*Designed specifically for balance confidence, knee osteotomy protection, and cervical post-fusion stability.*

### Tai Chi TC-BEG1: 10-Minute Seated Mindful Energy & Breath Flow
- **Duration**: 10 Minutes
- **Intensity**: Very Beginner / Seated
- **Equipment**: Sturdy Chair without arms
- **Focus**: Torso mobility, diaphragmatic coordination, shoulder relaxation.
- **Protocol**:
  1. **Commencing Form / Opening the Chest (3 mins)**: Sit tall, feet flat on floor. Inhale, float both hands slowly up to mid-chest height (elbows soft and low). Exhale, slowly press hands down toward lap. Repeat with smooth breath.
  2. **Wave Hands Like Clouds (Seated Variation) (4 mins)**: Sit upright, spine tall. Sweep right palm across chest from right to left while left hand rests low. Rotate through thoracic spine; keep head aligned with sternum (zero independent neck twisting). Reverse sides.
  3. **Closing the Circle / Harmonizing Qi (3 mins)**: Inhale, float arms wide in a gentle circle (below shoulder height). Exhale, bring palms together and lower toward lower dantian (lower belly). Sit quietly and breathe.

---

### Tai Chi TC-BEG2: 20-Minute Supported Standing Balance & Kinetic Grounding
- **Duration**: 20 Minutes
- **Intensity**: Very Beginner / Supported Standing
- **Equipment**: Sturdy Rail or Chair Back, Flat Supportive Shoes
- **Focus**: Weight shifting, ankle stability, knee alignment, vestibular balance.
- **Protocol**:
  1. **Stance Alignment & Weight Shifting (Holding Rail) (6 mins)**: Stand with feet shoulder-width apart, lightly touching pool rail or chair back. Slowly shift 70% of body weight to the right leg, then smoothly transition 70% to the left leg. Keep knees softly bent (never locked, no knee twist).
  2. **Repulse the Monkey (Modified Minimal Arm Reach) (6 mins)**: Lightly holding rail with one hand, step one foot back slowly with soft heel-to-toe contact. Push opposite hand forward below shoulder level. 3 mins per side.
  3. **Brush Knee & Push (Supported Stance) (5 mins)**: Holding rail, take a shallow step forward with the left foot. Sweep right hand gently forward in a pushing motion while left hand sweeps down near the hip. Maintain an upright, vertical spine. 10 reps per side.
  4. **Zhan Zhuang (Standing Like a Tree) Supported (3 mins)**: Stand with both hands lightly resting on the rail. Spine elongated, tailbone gently tucked, chin lightly retracted. Focus on deep rhythmic belly breathing.

---

### Tai Chi TC-BEG3: 30-Minute Restorative Flow for Chronic Pain & Vitality
- **Duration**: 30 Minutes
- **Intensity**: Very Beginner / Hybrid Seated & Standing
- **Equipment**: Sturdy Chair, Rail/Countertop
- **Focus**: Joint lubrication without wear, full kinetic integration, pain desensitization.
- **Protocol**:
  1. **Gentle Joint Loosening (Neck Neutral & Extremities) (6 mins)**: Seated or supported standing. Slow wrist circles, gentle ankle circles (unweighted), gentle shoulder rolls backward. Avoid any neck rolling.
  2. **Six Harmonies Upper Body Flow (8 mins)**: Seated upright. Coordinated flowing movements of the arms rising and sinking with breath. Focus on sinking the shoulders down away from the ears to relieve upper trapezius spasm.
  3. **Tai Chi Slow Walking with Rail Support (8 mins)**: Walk slowly alongside a pool rail or countertop. Place heel down first, roll foot smoothly through arch to toes, then shift weight deliberately. Turn slowly without pivoting sharply on the knee.
  4. **Parting the Wild Horse’s Mane (Supported Variation) (5 mins)**: Lightly touch rail. Diagonal step forward, gently expand front arm diagonally across torso keeping elbow bent and below shoulder height. 10 reps each side.
  5. **Calming the Waters (Closing Mindful Meditation) (3 mins)**: Return to seated. Rest hands over navel. Inhale calm, exhale release muscular holding patterns.

---
*Safety Note: Maintain light hand contact with a sturdy rail or chair during standing Tai Chi to guarantee balance safety.*
