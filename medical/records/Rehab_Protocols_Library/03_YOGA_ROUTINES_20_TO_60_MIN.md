# Floor-Based Yoga Routines (20–60 Minutes)

**Document**: `03_YOGA_ROUTINES_20_TO_60_MIN.md`  
**Governing Roles**: Physiotherapist (`PHYSIO`) & Surgeon (`SURGEON`)  
**Target Environment**: Cushioned Mat, Wall Support, Pillows/Bolsters  
**Target Equipment**: Yoga Mat, Bolster, Yoga Strap, Yoga Blocks, Resistance Bands, Acupressure Mat, Pilates Bar (optional).

---

## 1. Medium Intensity Protocols (20–40 Minutes)

### Y-MED1: 20-Minute Posture, Scapula & Core Alignment (Adapted Routine 28)
- **Duration**: 20–25 Minutes
- **Intensity**: Medium
- **Equipment**: Mat, Light Band, Wall
- **Focus**: Scapular retractions, neutral spine core recruitment, pelvic stability.
- **Protocol**:
  1. **Wall Angels (Partial Range)** (2 mins): Stand against wall, lower back flat, chin tucked. Slide arms up and down keeping elbows below 90° and wrists in contact.
  2. **Supported Scapular Retractions** (3 mins): Standing or seated upright. Squeeze shoulder blades together and down without elevating traps or shrugging.
  3. **Dead Bug (Arms Only or Heel Slides)** (3 mins): Lie supine, knees bent. Brace transverse abdominis. Alternating arm reaches or heel slides without arching lumbar spine.
  4. **Glute Bridges** (4 mins): 3 sets of 10 reps. Feet flat, drive through heels, lift hips into neutral extension. 45s rest between sets.
  5. **Alternating Heel Slides** (3 mins): Smooth controlled extension along the mat. Keep pelvis completely level.
  6. **Constructive Rest** (5–8 mins): Lie supine with bolster under knees. Slow diaphragmatic breathing.

---

### Y-MED2: 30-Minute Spine & Pelvic Stability Flow
- **Duration**: 30 Minutes
- **Intensity**: Medium
- **Equipment**: Mat, Yoga Strap, Bolster, Yoga Block
- **Focus**: Thoracic mobility without cervical rotation, hip stability, lumbar unloading.
- **Protocol**:
  1. **Pelvic Tilts & Transverse Holds** (5 mins): Supine, knees bent. Alternating gentle posterior pelvic tilts with 5-second isometric core brace holds.
  2. **Open Book Thoracic Mobilization** (5 mins): Side-lying, bolster clamped firmly between knees to lock pelvis. Sweep top arm open across the chest. Rotate only through mid-back; neck stays aligned with torso. 10 reps per side.
  3. **Thread the Needle (Modified Forearm-Supported)** (5 mins): Four-point position with cushioned knees or seated. Slide hand through without collapsing shoulder or twisting cervical spine. 10 reps per side.
  4. **Supported Bridge Holds** (5 mins): Place yoga block on lowest height under sacrum. Rest full pelvic weight onto block to decompress lumbar discs.
  5. **Supine Strap Hamstring & Calf Release** (5 mins): Loop yoga strap around ball of foot. Extend leg gently upwards without straining right shoulder grip. 2.5 mins per leg.
  6. **Supported Savasana** (5 mins): Mat rest with bolster under knees and small cervical support roll.

---

### Y-MED3: 40-Minute Restorative Decompression & Nerve Gliding
- **Duration**: 40 Minutes
- **Intensity**: Medium-Restorative
- **Equipment**: Mat, Bolster, 2 Blocks, Strap, Acupressure Mat
- **Focus**: C7–T1 nerve desensitization, neurodynamic gliding, hip decompression.
- **Protocol**:
  1. **Constructive Rest & Diaphragmatic Breathwork** (8 mins): Supine with knees over bolster. 4-second inhale into belly, 6-second exhale.
  2. **Supine AROM Cervical Rotation & Chin Tucks** (6 mins): Micro-retractions (chin tucks) on mat (10 reps). Gentle active rotation left and right within 50% pain-free range (10 reps).
  3. **Upper Limb Neurodynamic Sliders (Median & Ulnar)** (6 mins): Gentle nerve flossing with elbow bending and wrist extension. Keep arm below 80° elevation to prevent burning nerve spikes. 3 sets x 8 reps per arm.
  4. **Supine Figure-Four with Strap Assistance** (8 mins): Cross right ankle over left thigh. Loop strap around left hamstring to gently draw toward chest. 4 mins per side.
  5. **Supported Sphinx Micro-Lifts (Forearms Grounded)** (5 mins): Lie prone on mat. Rest weight on forearms, lifting upper chest a fraction of an inch while cervical spine remains strictly rigid and aligned with upper back.
  6. **Acupressure Mat Wind-Down** (7 mins): Supine transfer onto acupressure mat with bolster under knees.

## 2. Expert Strength & Rehabilitation Protocols (45–60 Minutes)

### Y-EXP1: 45-Minute Total Body Isometric Yoga & Pilates Hybrid
- **Duration**: 45 Minutes
- **Intensity**: Expert Isometric
- **Equipment**: Mat, Resistance Bands, Pilates Bar (or cords), Yoga Blocks
- **Focus**: High muscular tension without spinal flexion or joint impact.
- **Protocol**:
  1. **Supine Isometric Core Bracing & Pelvic Clocks** (8 mins): Engage deep core stabilizers in 12-to-6 and 3-to-9 o'clock pelvic tilt patterns against sustained manual resistance.
  2. **Dead Bug Complex with Resistance Band** (10 mins): Band looped around feet. Maintain strict 90° hip angle; press one heel down against band tension while stabilizing trunk. 3 sets x 10 reps per side.
  3. **Supine Pilates Bar Leg Press** (10 mins): Lie flat on mat, feet hooked into resistance cords attached to Pilates bar. Press bar away by extending knees, building quad and glute strength without knee joint shear. 4 sets x 15 reps.
  4. **Seated Low Rows with Band** (8 mins): Sit tall against a supportive wall, legs extended. Loop band around feet; row handles into lower ribs, keeping elbows low. 3 sets x 15 reps.
  5. **Supported Bridge Holds with Adductor Block Squeeze** (5 mins): Place yoga block between knees during bridge hold. Squeeze block to recruit adductors and deep pelvic floor. 3 sets of 45-second holds.
  6. **Final Supine Decompression** (4 mins): Bolster under knees, passive relaxation.

### Y-EXP2: 60-Minute Comprehensive Spinal Rehabilitation & Kinetic Strength
- **Duration**: 60 Minutes
- **Intensity**: Expert Strength & Stability
- **Equipment**: Mat, Resistance Bands, Bolster, Blocks, Light Dumbbells (1–2 kg)
- **Focus**: Total kinetic chain reinforcement from cervical stabilizers to ankle extensors.
- **Protocol**:
  1. **Phase 1: Deep Neck Flexor & Scapular Conditioning (10 mins)**:
     - Supine isometric chin tucks against resistance (3 sets x 10 reps, 5s hold).
     - Prone scapular retractions and W-raises with forehead resting on block (3 sets x 12 reps).
  2. **Phase 2: Scapulothoracic & Band Complex (12 mins)**:
     - Standing/seated band pull-aparts below shoulder height (3 sets x 15 reps).
     - Standing 30° dumbbell shrugs with light weights (3 sets x 10 reps).
     - Banded external and internal rotations with towel rolled under elbow (3 sets x 10 reps).
  3. **Phase 3: Core & Lumbopelvic Crucible (15 mins)**:
     - Banded Dead Bug with contralateral limb resistance (3 sets x 12 reps).
     - Quadruped knee hovers on cushioned mat (hands on blocks, lift knees 1 inch off mat, hold 30s x 3 sets).
     - Glute bridge pulses with loop band above knees (3 sets x 15 reps).
  4. **Phase 4: Hip & Lower Kinetic Chain Integration (12 mins)**:
     - Side-lying banded clamshells (left knee braced) (3 sets x 15 reps per leg).
     - Side-lying straight-leg abductions against band (3 sets x 12 reps per leg).
     - Supine single-leg pushdowns with resistance band (3 sets x 12 reps per leg).
  5. **Phase 5: Passive Decompression & Myofascial Unloading (11 mins)**:
     - Reclined Bound Angle Pose with blocks supporting outside of thighs (5 mins).
     - Constructive rest with bolster and slow diaphragmatic breathing (6 mins).

## 3. Classic Land Routines (Pages 6 & 7 Library)

- **Routine 28. Posture + Core (20–25 min)**: Wall angels partial range (2 min), Scapular retractions (3 min), Dead Bug arms only (2 min), Glute bridges (3 sets), Heel slides (3 min), Rest (7–10 min).
- **Routine 29. Recovery Session (20–30 min)**: 3–4 comfortable cleared stretches (6–8 min), Constructive rest with knees supported (8–12 min), Slow breathing (5–10 min).
- **Routine 30. Mixed Variety (25–30 min)**: Supported Hundred preparation (3 min, head down), Cat-Cow gentle (2 min if cleared), Glute bridges (3 sets), Dead Bug arms only (2 min), Figure-four (2 min each side), Hamstring stretch (2 min each side), Rest (6–10 min).

---
*Safety Note: Maintain cervical neutral alignment throughout all routines. Avoid any head elevation during supine core work unless supported by hands or a cushion.*
