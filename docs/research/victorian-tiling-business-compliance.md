# Victorian Tiling Business: Admin and Tax Compliance Primer

Research date: 2026-10-14. Ticket: `docs/wayfinder/rumble-oct-refresh/tickets/10-business-finance-research.md`.

> General information only, not tax, legal, or financial advice. Thresholds and rates change; re-check the cited primary source before relying on any figure. Engage a registered BAS agent or tax agent (verify on the TPB register) for lodgements on the business's behalf.

## 1. Scenario

Taking over admin/accounting for a friend's Victorian tiling business with GST turnover above $75,000, so GST registration is mandatory.

## 2. GST and BAS

- **Registration**: required once GST turnover is $75,000 or more. Source: https://www.ato.gov.au/businesses-and-organisations/gst-excise-and-indirect-taxes/gst/registering-for-gst
- **Reporting cycle**: businesses under $20 million GST turnover generally report quarterly (monthly optional; annual possible only if voluntarily registered under $75k). Source: https://www.ato.gov.au/businesses-and-organisations/preparing-lodging-and-paying/business-activity-statements-bas
- **Quarterly due dates** (self-lodged): Q1 Jul-Sep 28 Oct; Q2 Oct-Dec 28 Feb; Q3 Jan-Mar 28 Apr; Q4 Apr-Jun 28 Jul. Registered agents lodging electronically may get later concessional dates. Monthly BAS is due the 21st of the following month. Source: https://www.ato.gov.au/businesses-and-organisations/preparing-lodging-and-paying/business-activity-statements-bas/due-dates-for-lodging-and-paying-your-bas
- **Key labels**:
  - G1 total sales (GST-inclusive)
  - 1A GST on sales
  - 1B GST on purchases (credits claimable, needs valid tax invoices for purchases over $82.50 incl. GST)
  - W1 total salary, wages and other payments
  - W2 amounts withheld from W1
  - T7/T8/T9 or T11 PAYG instalment
  Source: https://www.ato.gov.au/businesses-and-organisations/preparing-lodging-and-paying/business-activity-statements-bas/completing-your-bas
- **Cash vs accrual**: businesses under $10 million aggregated turnover may account for GST on a cash basis (report when paid/received); otherwise accruals (when invoiced). Source: https://www.ato.gov.au/businesses-and-organisations/gst-excise-and-indirect-taxes/gst/accounting-for-gst-in-your-business/choosing-an-accounting-method
- Simpler BAS reporting applies to businesses under $10 million GST turnover (G1, 1A, 1B only for GST).

## 3. PAYG instalments and withholding

- **PAYG instalments**: prepayment of the business/owner's income tax, entered automatically once business/investment income passes ATO thresholds; paid via BAS or instalment notice (amount or rate option). Source: https://www.ato.gov.au/businesses-and-organisations/preparing-lodging-and-paying/payg-instalments
- **PAYG withholding**: must register before first paying employees, or paying contractors who fail to quote an ABN (withhold at top marginal rate, currently 47%). Reported at W1/W2. Source: https://www.ato.gov.au/businesses-and-organisations/hiring-and-paying-your-workers/payg-withholding

## 4. Taxable Payments Annual Report (TPAR)

- Businesses mainly in building and construction (tiling qualifies) that pay contractors for building and construction services must lodge TPAR by **28 August** each year, covering the prior financial year. Record each contractor's ABN, name, address, gross paid and GST. Source: https://www.ato.gov.au/businesses-and-organisations/preparing-lodging-and-paying/reports-and-returns/taxable-payments-annual-report

## 5. Employees: super guarantee and Single Touch Payroll

- **Super guarantee rate**: 12% from 1 July 2025 (final legislated step). Source: https://www.ato.gov.au/businesses-and-organisations/super-for-employers
- **Payday Super** (from 1 July 2026): SG must be paid with each pay run and generally received by the fund within 7 business days, replacing quarterly payment. Calculated on "qualifying earnings". Source: https://www.ato.gov.au/businesses-and-organisations/super-for-employers/payday-super
- **Single Touch Payroll**: mandatory for all employers; pay, tax and super reported to the ATO each pay day from STP-enabled software. Source: https://www.ato.gov.au/businesses-and-organisations/hiring-and-paying-your-workers/single-touch-payroll
- Contractors paid mainly for labour can be employees for super purposes even if they hold an ABN.

## 6. Contractor vs employee

- Determined by the whole relationship under the contract (right to control, ability to delegate/subcontract, who bears risk, paid for result vs time, own tools). Having an ABN does not settle it. Use the ATO employee/contractor decision tool. Source: https://www.ato.gov.au/businesses-and-organisations/hiring-and-paying-your-workers/employee-or-contractor
- Misclassification risks unpaid SG (plus SG charge), PAYG withholding, WorkCover premiums and payroll tax liabilities.

## 7. WorkSafe Victoria (WorkCover) insurance

- Must register if the business pays or expects to pay more than **$7,500 rateable remuneration** a financial year, or employs any apprentice or trainee regardless of amount. Sole traders with no workers do not need it (consider personal accident/income protection). Register within 60 days of becoming liable. Deemed workers (some contractors) can count. Source: https://www.worksafe.vic.gov.au/do-i-need-workcover-insurance

## 8. Victorian payroll tax

- 2025-26: tax-free threshold **$1,000,000** annual ($83,333 monthly); general rate 4.85%, regional rate 1.2125%; threshold phases out for national wages $3m-$5m. Unlikely to apply to a small tiling crew, but grouping rules and contractor wages can bring payments in. Source: https://www.sro.vic.gov.au/payroll-tax-rates-and-thresholds (as of 2026-10-14; verify 2026-27 figures).

## 9. Record keeping

- Keep records for **5 years** from when prepared/obtained or the transaction completed (longer if in dispute). Records must be in English (or easily converted), digital accepted. Include tax invoices, bank statements, payroll, TPAR contractor details, vehicle logbooks. Source: https://www.ato.gov.au/businesses-and-organisations/preparing-lodging-and-paying/record-keeping-for-business
- Victorian guidance on bookkeeping basics: https://business.vic.gov.au/business-information/finance-and-accounting

## 10. Access: Relationship Authorisation Manager (RAM)

- The business owner (principal authority) sets up RAM with a myID (Standard or Strong strength) and authorises the helper as an "authorisation administrator" or "standard user" with scoped permissions; this gives Online services for business access. Do not share the owner's myID. Source: https://www.ato.gov.au/online-services/access-and-manage-online-services-for-business/relationship-authorisation-manager
- An authorised staff member may lodge for that business in Online services for business; that is different from providing services as a third party for a fee (see section 11).

## 11. Legal limit on providing BAS services

- Under the *Tax Agent Services Act 2009*, a person who provides a **BAS service for a fee or other reward** must be a registered BAS agent or tax agent. BAS services include advising on or preparing/lodging BAS-related matters (GST, PAYG W/I, FBT instalments, payroll-related ATO obligations including STP and SG) where the client is expected to rely on it. Source: https://www.tpb.gov.au/bas-services and https://www.tpb.gov.au/do-i-need-register
- Generally exempt: an employee of the business acting for their employer, and genuinely unpaid help. Payment in kind (e.g. free tiling work) can count as "other reward". Bookkeeping data entry under the supervision of a registered agent is permitted.
- Practical recommendation: if the helper is not an employee and receives any reward, either register with the TPB (requires qualifications plus experience) or work under a registered BAS/tax agent who signs off lodgements. Check the TPB register: https://www.tpb.gov.au/public-register
