# Chemist Warehouse Complaint & ACCC Report Record

**Date:** 13 October 2026  
**Subject:** Chemist Warehouse - Unauthorised Script Cancellations & Service Failure  

---

## 1. Summary of Incident

* **Entity:** Chemist Warehouse
* **Issue Type:** Service Failure / Breach of Australian Consumer Law (Section 60 - Failure to provide service with due care and skill)
* **Core Issue:** 
  * Active, valid medical prescriptions were cancelled repeatedly without patient notification, consultation, or consent.
  * Zero communication provided (no SMS, email, or telephone contact prior to cancellation).
  * In-person presentations to the pharmacy counter were met with an inability or refusal to assist, resolve the cancellation, or provide vital medications.
  * Severe impact on continuity of care and health access.
