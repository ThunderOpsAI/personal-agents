# Budget Analytics & Monthly Overviews
Labels: wayfinder:prototype

## The Goal
Design and prototype the calendar-month grouped Weekly Budget Overview hub, including highly interactive visualizations and reports.

## Context
When clicking the "SPENT 120" button, it should navigate to the Budget section. The user explicitly requested: "calendar months and the most interactvie visuals available. better is better."

## Requirements
- Entry point: Clicking the "SPENT" badge on the main dashboard.
- Display a list of weekly overviews grouped/filed into calendar months.
- Include print-ready reports, graphs, and analytics.
- Evaluate and use a top-tier charting library (e.g., Recharts, Chart.js) to deliver highly interactive and accessible data visuals.
- Do a throwaway prototype first to align on the visual fidelity of the charts before fully wiring up the backend if necessary.
