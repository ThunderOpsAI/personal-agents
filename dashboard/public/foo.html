⚠️ CRITICAL FOR JAMIE: Crown Casino Signup Notice

Because you are using an Australian Passport, Crown Casino REQUIRES a secondary document showing your current residential address!

Australian passports do NOT display an address. You MUST bring one physical original document:

  • Recent Utility Bill (Power, Gas, or Water within 3 months)
  • Bank Statement with your address (within 3 months)
  • Centrelink or ATO Official Letter (within 12 months)
  • Physical Driver’s Licence or Proof of Age Card

*Screenshots or phone photos are NOT accepted at Crown verification desks.
