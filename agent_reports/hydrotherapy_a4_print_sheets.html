Cervical Fusion (C4–C7): Neutral spine strictly locked; no head thrusting.
Right Shoulder: Below 80° abduction.
Left Knee: Max 45° flexion.
Water: 32°C–34°C ideal.
h1 Buoyancy Spinal Decompression
25 MIN • RESTORATIVE DECOMPRESSION
Target: Lumbar disc & facet offload, C4–C7 axial decompression Equipment: Deep water noodle, cervical flotation collar Mechanism: Eliminates 90% gravity compression
1. Buoyant Vertical Traction6m 15s
Place noodle under armpits in chest/deep water so feet hang completely suspended. Relax abdominal wall and let legs dangle. Zero foot contact. Breathe deeply into diaphragm.
2. Gentle Water Knee-to-Chest6m 15s
While vertically suspended, slowly draw both knees towards chest. Hold 3s to open posterior facet joints. Lower with control. 3 sets x 10 smooth reps.
3. Aquatic Torso Pendulum6m 15s
Holding noodle securely, let legs and pelvis sway smoothly side to side like a gentle pendulum. Keep head and cervical spine completely still and aligned.
4. Supine Water Float & Reset6m 15s
Transition onto back with cervical pillow and knee noodle bolster. Complete passive floating surrender. Slow 4s inhale, 6s exhale to extinguish autonomic pain loops.
h2 Aquatic Multi-Planar Gait & Walking
20 MIN • ADAPTIVE MOBILITY & GAIT RETRAINING
Target: Symmetrical stride length, gluteus medius, hip flexor activation Equipment: Chest-deep pool lane, pool rail Focus: Upright posture, no forward trunk flexion
1. Forward Water Marching5m 00s
Chest-deep water. Drive knee upward smoothly (max 70° hip flexion) with conscious heel-to-toe rolling push-off. Maintain upright chest and natural reciprocal arm swing submerged.
2. Backward Heel-to-Toe Walking5m 00s
Step backward toe-to-heel. Feel the drag of water fire hamstrings and glutes without lumbar hyperextension. Maintain level pelvis throughout stride.
3. Lateral Sidestepping5m 00s
Face pool edge. Step sideways across lane, pushing against water column with outer hip. Step wide, close slowly. Fires gluteus medius to stabilize pelvis & SI joint.
4. Warm Water Calming Wall Rest5m 00s
Stand supported with back against pool wall. Hands on rail. Feel hydrostatic pressure support venous return. Gentle deep breathing before exiting.

h3 Aquatic Hip & Core Stabilization
20 MIN • TARGETED STRENGTHENING
1. Aquatic Standing Hip Abduction5m 00s
Stand holding pool rail for balance. Keep torso tall and sweep leg laterally out against water resistance. 3 sets x 12 reps per leg. Controlled tempo; avoid pelvic hiking.
2. Kickboard Core Press-Down5m 00s
Hold kickboard flat with both hands at chest level. Engage deep transversus abdominis and push board straight down into water column. 3 sets x 10 reps (3s hold at bottom).
3. Aquatic Bicycle Pedaling5m 00s
Lean back resting upper back on noodle across armpits. Smoothly pedal legs through water column like riding a bicycle. Maintain rhythmic cadence without knee shear.
4. Floating Spine Rest & Elongation5m 00s
Supported supine float with noodle under knees. Lengthen spine, relax pelvic floor and deep hip rotators.
h4 Warm Water Relaxation & Lymphatic Flush
20 MIN • LYMPHATIC & AUTONOMIC FLUSH
1. Deep Water Submersion Breathing5m 00s
Submerged to clavicles. Inhale 4s through nose; let water hydrostatic pressure assist full, effortless 6s exhalation. Lowers systemic vascular resistance.
2. Aquatic Arm Sweeps (Lymphatic Flow)5m 00s
Slow figure-eight arm sweeps submerged below chest line. Elbows soft, hands relaxed. Drives axillary lymphatic drainage without shoulder impingement.
3. Ankle & Foot Water Mobility5m 00s
Rest hands on rail at pool steps. Point, flex, and rotate ankles through full fluid range. Activates calf venous muscle pump to clear peripheral fluid.
4. Supported Supine Water Float5m 00s
Complete stillness on water surface with neck pillow and knee noodle. Deep parasympathetic reset to downregulate dorsal horn pain gating.

H-MED1 Dynamic Aqua Balance & Banded Core
30 MIN • MEDIUM RESISTANCE
Equipment: Medium Theraband (Green), Pool Rail, Noodle Focus: Pelvic stability, anti-shear core bracing, gait power
Phase 1: Dynamic Aquatic Warm-Up5m 00s
Forward walking (heel-to-toe) in chest-deep water (2m) • Backward walking engaging hamstrings/glutes (2m) • Lateral side-stepping holding pool rail (1m).
Phase 2: Banded Hip Abduction & Extension8m 00s
Anchor band to rail; loop to ankle. Stand tall holding rail. Abduction: sweep leg out against band/water tension (3x12/leg). Extension: sweep straight back into glutes without arching lumbar spine (3x12/leg).
Phase 3: Pool Wall Pelvic Neutral & Wall Press7m 00s
Back flat against wall. Flatten lower spine into wall by bracing deep core (hold 10s x 6 reps). Submerge noodle at chest level; press downward engaging lats without flexing neck (3x10 reps).
Phase 4: Noodle Deep Water Core Suspensions6m 00s
Noodle under axillae in deep water. Alternate slow knee lifts toward chest against water resistance (3 sets x 15 reps). Keep torso completely vertical; no swinging.
Phase 5: Buoyancy Decompression Cool-Down4m 00s
Supported supine float with noodle under knees and neck collar. Parasympathetic reset: diaphragmatic breathing, feeling warm water drain tension from spine.
H-MED2 Total Body Aquatic Conditioning & Scapular Rowing
45 MIN • MEDIUM RESISTANCE
Equipment: Heavy Theraband, Pool Rail, Kickboard, Water Dumbbells Focus: Upper back scapular stabilization, shallow squats without knee shear
Phase 1: Hydrodynamic Gait & Scapular Prep10m 00s
Forward, backward, and lateral water strides (5m). Submerged arm sweeps and aquatic chin retractions in chest-deep water (5m). Activates deep cervical flexors safely.
Phase 2: Submerged Rail Band Rows & W-Rows10m 00s
Anchor heavy band to rail at waist height. Low Row: Pull handles back, squeezing shoulder blades together (elbows strictly below shoulder) (3x12). W-Row: Flare hands slightly, squeeze lower traps (3x10).
Phase 3: Banded Pool-Rail Shallow Squat Presses10m 00s
Heavy band around rail and pelvis. Facing rail, sink into shallow water squat (max 45° knee bend). Drive up forcefully through heels and glutes against band pull. 4 sets x 10 reps.
Phase 4: Kickboard Aquatic Core Presses & Twists10m 00s
Submerge kickboard vertically with both hands; press down into water column (3x12). Gentle torso rotations with board submerged (eyes strictly forward, cervical locked) (3x10).
Phase 5: Decompression & Ankle Flushes5m 00s
Hold rail at pool step. Perform slow ankle circles, calf stretches on pool step edge, and passive buoyant decompression float.

Poolside Safety Rules & Biomechanical Restrictions
1. Cervical Neutrality C4–C7 anterior fusion hardware requires zero cervical flexion or extension during exertion. Never thrust chin forward against water resistance. Keep gaze locked forward on the horizon.
2. Shoulder Protection Right shoulder exercises must remain below 80° abduction/flexion. Zero overhead presses or wide flyes. Keep elbows tucked low near ribcage during all pulling and pushing movements.
3. Knee Joint Care Left knee meniscal preservation: max 45° knee flexion during squats and marches. Avoid sudden pivoting or twisting on a planted foot. Smooth fluid transitions only.
EMERGENCY STOP & EXIT CRITERIA: Stop session immediately and safely exit pool if you experience: (1) Sudden electric or shooting pain down either arm or leg; (2) Dizziness, lightheadedness, or overheating from pool warmth; (3) Chest tightness or shortness of breath; (4) Numbness in hands or feet. Hydrate with electrolytes post-pool.
