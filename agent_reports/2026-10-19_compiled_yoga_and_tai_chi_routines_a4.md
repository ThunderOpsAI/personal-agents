# Compiled Clinical Yoga & Tai Chi Protocols (A4 Laminated Series)

**Document Reference:** `agent_reports/2026-10-19_compiled_yoga_and_tai_chi_routines_a4.md`  
**Governing Roles:** Physiotherapist (`PHYSIO`), Neuro-PPS (`NEURO_PPS`), Rehabilitation Specialist  
**Target Biomechanics & Restrictions:**
- **Cervical Spine (ACDF C4–C7 Post-Fusion):** Strictly neutral cervical spine; zero end-range rotation or forced extension; supported head/neck cushion during all floor and supine postures.
- **Left Knee (Meniscal Repair / Protection):** Knee flexion &le;45&deg;; strictly avoid unpadded kneeling; provide double-thick bolster or towel support under knees during quadruped poses.
- **Right Shoulder (Subacromial Decompression & Rotator Cuff Protection):** Active arm elevation &le;80&deg;; elbows kept below clavicular plane; avoidance of aggressive overhead reaches or weight bearing on fully extended arms.

---

> ### Medical & Clinical Disclaimer
> *This clinical documentation provides decision support and rehabilitation protocols synthesized for personal reference and guided physical therapy. It does not constitute independent medical diagnosis or prescription. All exercises must be cleared by your treating neurosurgeon, orthopedic surgeon, and physiotherapist. If sharp pain, radicular symptoms, dizziness, or peripheral paresthesia occur, stop immediately, safely rest in constructive rest position, and seek prompt clinician evaluation.*

## Detailed Protocols by Category

### Category 1: Beginner Yoga Flows (Sheet 1)

#### Flow Y-BEG1: 10-Minute Morning Gentle Reset (10 min)
- **Objective:** Awakening circulation and resetting lumbar curvature before standing.
- **Equipment:** Mat, Bolster/Pillow
- **Steps:**
  1. **Constructive Rest Position (3 min):** Lie supine with bolster under knees. Hands on belly. Deep diaphragmatic 4-7 breathing.
  2. **Supine Pelvic Tilts (3 min):** Inhale natural lumbar arch. Exhale flatten lumbar spine smoothly into mat without buttock lifting. 10 slow reps.
  3. **Alternating Heel Slides (2 min):** Slowly extend right heel along floor, slide back. Repeat left. Keep pelvis completely level.
  4. **Diaphragmatic Centering Breath (2 min):** Hands on ribcage, 360° expansion, relaxing neck, jaw, and facial muscles.

#### Flow Y-BEG2: 20-Minute Restorative Hip & Lumbar Unwind (20 min)
- **Objective:** Unloading pelvic tightness, piriformis release, gentle hip mobility.
- **Equipment:** Mat, Bolster, Yoga Strap
- **Steps:**
  1. **Supported Child's Pose over Bolster (5 min):** Wide knees, large bolster between thighs. Fold forward, chest and head resting comfortably.
  2. **Supine Single Knee-to-Chest (5 min):** Hug right hamstring (avoid knee cap) towards chest. Hold 2.5 min per leg. Relax sacrum flat.
  3. **Supine Figure-Four with Strap (5 min):** Right ankle over left thigh. Loop strap under left thigh to gently assist. 2.5 min each side.
  4. **Supported Savasana (5 min):** Full supine rest with bolster under knees, arms at sides, palms facing upward.

#### Flow Y-BEG3: 30-Minute Full-Body Decompression & Downregulation (30 min)
- **Objective:** Autonomic nervous system regulation, vagal tone, cervical relaxation.
- **Equipment:** Mat, Bolster, Yoga Block, Small Head Pillow
- **Steps:**
  1. **Constructive Rest with Vagal Breath (6 min):** Bolster under knees. Inhale 4s through nose, smooth 7s exhale through soft pursed lips.
  2. **Supine Cervical Rotation & Chin Tucks (5 min):** 10 gentle chin retractions against mat; 10 slow comfortable head turns left/right.
  3. **Open Book Thoracic Mobilization (7 min):** Side-lying with cushion between knees. Sweep top arm open across chest. 10 reps each side.
  4. **Supported Bridge on Lowest Block (6 min):** Yoga block at lowest height under sacrum. Full pelvic weight supported, opening hip flexors.
  5. **Legs-Elevated Wall Relaxation (6 min):** Calves elevated onto couch or wall bolster. Hands on lower belly, relaxing lower back.

### Category 2: Mindful Tai Chi Protocols (Sheet 2)

#### Tai Chi TC-BEG1: 10-Minute Seated Mindful Energy & Breath Flow (10 min)
- **Objective:** Torso mobility, diaphragmatic coordination, shoulder relaxation.
- **Equipment:** Sturdy Chair without arms
- **Steps:**
  1. **Commencing Form / Opening the Chest (3 min):** Sit tall, feet grounded. Inhale float palms up to mid-chest with soft elbows. Exhale press down gently.
  2. **Wave Hands Like Clouds (Seated) (4 min):** Sweep right palm across torso while left rests low. Rotate through thoracic spine, neck neutral.
  3. **Closing Circle / Harmonizing Qi (3 min):** Sweep arms wide in gentle low arc. Bring palms together and sink towards lower dantian (navel).

#### Tai Chi TC-BEG2: 20-Minute Supported Standing Balance & Kinetic Grounding (20 min)
- **Objective:** Weight shifting, ankle stability, knee alignment, vestibular balance.
- **Equipment:** Sturdy Rail or Chair Back, Flat Supportive Shoes
- **Steps:**
  1. **Stance Alignment & Weight Shifting (6 min):** Hands lightly touching rail or chair back. Shift 70% weight right, then left. Knees soft, no twist.
  2. **Repulse the Monkey (Modified) (6 min):** Touch rail with one hand. Step back softly heel-to-toe, push opposite palm forward below shoulder level.
  3. **Brush Knee & Push (Supported) (5 min):** Holding rail, shallow step forward with left foot. Sweep right hand in gentle push, spine vertical.
  4. **Zhan Zhuang (Standing Like a Tree) (3 min):** Both hands resting on rail. Tailbone gently tucked, crown reaching tall, diaphragmatic breath.

#### Tai Chi TC-BEG3: 30-Minute Restorative Flow for Chronic Pain & Vitality (30 min)
- **Objective:** Joint lubrication without wear, full kinetic integration, pain desensitization.
- **Equipment:** Sturdy Chair, Rail/Countertop
- **Steps:**
  1. **Gentle Joint Loosening (Neck Neutral) (6 min):** Slow wrist circles, unweighted ankle circles, gentle backwards shoulder rolls. Avoid neck circles.
  2. **Six Harmonies Upper Body Flow (8 min):** Synchronize rising and sinking arm movements with breath, sinking traps away from ears.
  3. **Tai Chi Slow Walking with Rail Support (8 min):** Walk slowly along rail. Place heel first, roll through midfoot to toes, smooth deliberate weight transfer.
  4. **Parting Wild Horse's Mane (Supported) (5 min):** Light rail contact. Diagonal step, gently expand front arm across torso with bent elbow below shoulder.
  5. **Calming the Waters (Closing Meditation) (3 min):** Seated upright, hands resting over lower abdomen. Deep rhythmic belly breathing, releasing tension.

### Category 3: Clinical Floor Yoga Protocols (Sheet 3)

#### Flow Y-MED1: 20-Minute Posture, Scapula & Core Alignment (25 min)
- **Objective:** Scapular retractions, neutral spine core recruitment, pelvic stability.
- **Equipment:** Mat, Light Band, Wall
- **Steps:**
  1. **Wall Angels (Partial Range) (2 min):** Stand against wall, lumbar flat, chin tucked. Slide arms keeping elbows below 90°.
  2. **Supported Scapular Retractions (3 min):** Standing or seated upright. Squeeze shoulder blades together and down without shrugging.
  3. **Dead Bug (Arms Only or Heel Slides) (3 min):** Supine, knees bent, core braced. Alternate gentle arm raises or heel slides without arching lower back.
  4. **Glute Bridges (4 min):** 3 sets of 10 reps. Drive through heels, lift hips into neutral alignment, squeeze glutes at top.
  5. **Alternating Heel Slides (3 min):** Controlled leg extension along mat. Keep pelvis level and transverse abdominis engaged.
  6. **Constructive Rest (5 min):** Supine with bolster under knees. Slow diaphragmatic breathing for parasympathetic reset.

#### Flow Y-MED2: 30-Minute Spine & Pelvic Stability Flow (30 min)
- **Objective:** Thoracic mobility without cervical rotation, hip stability, lumbar unloading.
- **Equipment:** Mat, Yoga Strap, Bolster, Yoga Block
- **Steps:**
  1. **Pelvic Tilts & Transverse Holds (5 min):** Supine, knees bent. Alternating gentle posterior pelvic tilts with 5-second core brace holds.
  2. **Open Book Thoracic Mobilization (5 min):** Side-lying, bolster clamped firmly between knees. Sweep top arm open; neck stays aligned.
  3. **Thread the Needle (Modified Forearm) (5 min):** Cushioned quadruped or seated. Slide arm through without collapsing shoulder or twisting cervical spine.
  4. **Supported Bridge Holds (5 min):** Yoga block at lowest height under sacrum. Rest full pelvic weight into block to decompress discs.
  5. **Supine Strap Hamstring & Calf Release (5 min):** Loop strap around ball of foot. Extend leg gently upwards without straining grip. 2.5 min each leg.
  6. **Supported Savasana (5 min):** Mat rest with bolster under knees and small cervical support roll under neck.

#### Flow Y-MED3: 40-Minute Restorative Decompression & Nerve Gliding (40 min)
- **Objective:** C7–T1 nerve desensitization, neurodynamic gliding, hip decompression.
- **Equipment:** Mat, Bolster, 2 Blocks, Strap, Acupressure Mat
- **Steps:**
  1. **Constructive Rest & Diaphragmatic Breath (8 min):** Supine with knees over bolster. 4-second inhale into belly, 6-second slow exhale.
  2. **Supine Cervical Rotation & Chin Tucks (6 min):** Micro-retractions against mat (10 reps); gentle rotation left/right within 50% comfortable arc.
  3. **Upper Limb Neurodynamic Sliders (6 min):** Gentle nerve flossing with elbow flex/extension and wrist gliding below 80° elevation.
  4. **Supine Figure-Four with Strap (8 min):** Cross ankle over opposite knee. Loop strap around hamstring to draw gently. 4 min per side.
  5. **Supported Sphinx Micro-Lifts (5 min):** Prone, forearms grounded. Lift sternum fraction of an inch; cervical spine rigid and aligned with back.
  6. **Acupressure Mat Wind-Down (7 min):** Supine on acupressure mat with bolster under knees. Deep relaxed breathing.

### Category 4: Expert Strength Yoga & Pilates Isometrics (Sheet 4)

#### Flow Y-EXP1: 45-Minute Total Body Isometric Yoga & Pilates Hybrid (45 min)
- **Objective:** High muscular tension without spinal flexion or joint impact.
- **Equipment:** Mat, Resistance Bands, Pilates Bar, Yoga Blocks
- **Steps:**
  1. **Supine Isometric Core Bracing & Pelvic Clocks (8 min):** Deep transverse abdominis engagement in 12-to-6 and 3-to-9 o'clock pelvic tilt patterns.
  2. **Dead Bug Complex with Resistance Band (10 min):** Band looped around feet, strict 90° hip angle. Press heel down against band while stabilizing core.
  3. **Supine Pilates Bar Leg Press (10 min):** Supine, feet in resistance cords of Pilates bar. Press away extending knees; quad/glute strength without knee shear.
  4. **Seated Low Rows with Band (8 min):** Sit tall against wall, band looped around feet. Row handles to lower ribs, elbows tucked low and back.
  5. **Supported Bridge Holds with Block Squeeze (5 min):** Yoga block between knees. Bridge up, isometric squeeze to recruit adductors and deep pelvic floor.
  6. **Final Supine Decompression (4 min):** Bolster under knees, complete passive recovery and parasympathetic downshift.

#### Flow Y-EXP2: 60-Minute Comprehensive Spinal Rehab & Kinetic Strength (60 min)
- **Objective:** Total kinetic chain reinforcement from cervical stabilizers to ankle extensors.
- **Equipment:** Mat, Resistance Bands, Bolster, Blocks, Light Dumbbells (1–2 kg)
- **Steps:**
  1. **Phase 1: Deep Neck Flexor & Scapular Conditioning (10 min):** Supine isometric chin tucks (3x10 reps, 5s hold); prone W-raises with forehead on block (3x12).
  2. **Phase 2: Scapulothoracic & Band Complex (12 min):** Band pull-aparts below shoulder height (3x15); 30° dumbbell shrugs (3x10); banded rotator cuff rotations.
  3. **Phase 3: Core & Lumbopelvic Crucible (15 min):** Banded Dead Bug with contralateral resistance (3x12); quadruped knee hovers 1 inch off mat (3x30s); bridge pulses.
  4. **Phase 4: Hip & Lower Kinetic Chain Integration (12 min):** Side-lying banded clamshells (left knee braced, 3x15); straight-leg abductions; supine single-leg pushdowns.
  5. **Phase 5: Passive Decompression & Myofascial Unloading (11 min):** Reclined Bound Angle Pose with blocks under thighs (5 min); constructive rest with bolster (6 min).
