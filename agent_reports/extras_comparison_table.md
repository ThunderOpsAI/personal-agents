### Strategy Breakdown for Your Rehab Needs:

1. **The "Double-Dip" Winners (Australian Unity & nib):** Because you need extensive rehab for the ankle, knee, and shoulder, you want to see a physiotherapist *and* a chiropractor/osteopath without them cannibalizing each other's limits. **Australian Unity** gives you $800 for Physio + $800 for Chiro = **$1,600** total. **nib** gives you $800 + $600 = **$1,400**. This perfectly matches your memory of a ~$1,500 split policy.
2. **The "Single Therapy" Winners (Bupa & HCF):** If you decided to *only* see a Physiotherapist and drop Chiro altogether, Bupa or HCF would be better because you could dump the entire $1,100 - $1,200 combined limit into just Physio. 
3. **Massage and Naturopathy:** Almost all funds (even on Top Extras) strictly cap "Natural Therapies" (which includes Remedial Massage, Naturopathy, Acupuncture, etc.) at around **$300 - $400 combined**. You will not find a $800 limit for massage alone.

**Next Step:** To lock in the absolute accurate price, you will need to punch your exact age and state into the **Australian Unity** or **nib** quoting tools on their websites, as base premiums vary slightly by state.
