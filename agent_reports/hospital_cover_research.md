# Private Hospital Cover for Joint Replacements (Victoria)

## Overview
This report compares Australian private health insurance hospital covers for **knee replacement and shoulder surgery** (classified under the clinical category of **Joint Replacements**). 
The comparison assumes a **single adult living in Victoria**. 

**Crucial Tier Requirement:** Under the Australian standardized clinical categories, standard "Silver" policies *do not* cover joint replacements. To be covered for a knee or shoulder replacement, you must hold either a **Silver Plus** policy (where the insurer explicitly adds joint replacements back in) or a **Gold** tier policy.

## Is Frank the Cheapest?
Based on the tiering requirements, **Frank Health Insurance** remains one of the cheapest options on the market because they offer a "Silver Plus Max" policy that specifically includes joint replacements without forcing the user to pay for a full "Gold" policy (which includes expensive categories like pregnancy and birth). At approximately **$82.75/week**, Frank is highly competitive compared to the major funds (Bupa, Medibank, HCF) whose equivalent covers often exceed $95-$110/week.

## Next Steps for the User
1. **Wait Periods:** A standard 12-month waiting period applies for pre-existing conditions and major surgeries like joint replacements.
2. **Out-of-Pocket Gaps:** Even with Gold/Silver Plus cover, surgeons and anaesthetists may charge above the Medicare Benefit Schedule (MBS) fee. 
3. **Verify via Government Site:** For the absolute to-the-dollar cheapest cover, the user should enter their exact date of birth and income into **[PrivateHealth.gov.au](https://www.privatehealth.gov.au)** and filter by "Joint Replacements".
