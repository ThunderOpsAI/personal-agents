# Health Insurance Research: Hospital and Extras Cover

## 1. Hospital Cover Needs
To ensure full coverage for your requested surgeries, we must map them to the Australian Government's standardized clinical categories and their corresponding minimum tiers.

### Clinical Categories & Minimum Tiers
*   **Ankle Fusion**: Falls under **"Bone, joint and muscle"**. Minimum tier: **Bronze**.
*   **Knee Metal Removal**: Also classified under **"Bone, joint and muscle"** (as follow-up hardware removal falls under the site of the original surgery). Minimum tier: **Bronze**.
*   **Shoulder Surgery**:
    *   If it is a repair/reconstruction (e.g., rotator cuff repair): **"Joint reconstructions"**. Minimum tier: **Bronze/Silver**.
    *   If it involves partial or full arthroplasty (replacement): **"Joint replacements"**. Minimum tier: **Gold**.
*   **Pain Interventions (e.g., via Dr. Brett Toddhunter)**:
    *   Standard injections/blocks (no implant): **"Pain management"**. Minimum tier: **Bronze/Silver**.
    *   Implants (e.g., neurostimulators, infusion pumps): **"Pain management with device"**. Minimum tier: **Gold**.

### Is Gold Cover Necessary?
**Yes, Gold is highly recommended—and potentially strictly necessary—depending on the exact procedures.**
*   If Dr. Toddhunter recommends **"Pain management with device"**, a **Gold** policy is the only standard tier mandated to cover this on an unrestricted basis. Silver and Bronze policies usually exclude or restrict it, leading to massive out-of-pocket hospital costs.
*   If your shoulder surgery requires a **joint replacement**, Gold is also mandated. 
*   *Comparison:* Your current **Medibank Gold** cover safely includes these unrestricted. **Frank Health** (and other budget funds) offer excellent value on Bronze/Silver tiers, but if you drop to a standard Frank Silver or Bronze policy, you will likely lose cover for "Pain management with device". Some funds offer "Silver Plus" which *might* include this, but you must explicitly verify the "Pain management with device" category on the Product Disclosure Statement (PDS) before downgrading.

## 2. Extras Cover: High Limits for Physio and Chiro
You recalled a policy with approximately $1,700/year for physio/chiro. In the current market, individual limits of $1,700 for a single modality are extremely rare per person. However, many "Top Extras" covers bundle physical therapies into a high **combined annual limit**, or offer family limits. 

Here is how the top funds generally structure their highest physical therapy limits:

*   **Medibank (Top Extras):** Does not usually bundle them. Typcal Top Extras offer ~$700 for Physiotherapy and ~$500 for Chiropractic (Total individual physical therapy limits ~$1,200/year).
*   **Bupa (Top Extras 60/75/90):** Limits vary by exact package, but they frequently use combined limits for natural/physical therapies (often $800 - $1,200+ depending on whether it's a family or single policy). Bupa shines if you use their "Members First" network for high percentage returns (up to 90% back per visit).
*   **AHM (Super Extras):** Provides a strict ~$600 annual limit for Physiotherapy. Chiropractic is capped similarly. Total combined value falls short of $1,700.
*   **Frank Health (Max/Top Extras):** Uses shared sub-limits. E.g., Chiro is shared with Osteo (often $400 single / $800 family), and Physio is shared with exercise physiology/hydrotherapy. 
*   **HCF (Top Extras):** Groups treatments into a combined physical therapies limit. They also offer a "More for Muscles/Backs" program which fully covers your initial consultation, preserving your annual limits for follow-ups.

**Recommendation for Extras:** If you are chasing a $1,700 limit, you are likely looking for a Top Extras package that provides a **"Combined Physical Therapies" limit** (e.g., rolling Physio, Chiro, Osteo, and Myotherapy into one large bucket) on a family or couples policy. Check Medibank's "Top Extras 90" or HCF's "Top Extras", but ensure you use their preferred provider networks (Members' Choice / No-Gap) to maximize the dollar value of each visit.
