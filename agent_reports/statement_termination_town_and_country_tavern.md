# Contemporaneous Witness Statement: Termination & Defamatory Conduct

**Witness / Complainant:** James Jones  
**Date of Statement:** 4 September 2026  
**Subject:** Unlawful Termination, Defamation, Malicious Blackballing, and Unpaid Wages  
**Employer / Respondent:** Town and Country Tavern, Wangaratta VIC  
**Status:** Confidential Legal Document / Evidence Record  

---

## 1. Background & Working Relationship
1. I was employed at the Town and Country Tavern in Wangaratta.
2. Despite being employed for a relatively short duration, the employer frequently sought my professional guidance and business perspective due to my extensive background in business management.
3. Approximately 2–3 days prior to my sudden termination, the employer drove me in her vehicle to a local golf course to deliver alcohol. During this journey, she spoke with me at length regarding the trials and tribulations of her businesses and repeatedly expressed trust in my insight.
4. I worked my regular scheduled shift the day prior to my termination with zero formal or informal performance warnings, complaints, or negative feedback raised against me.

---

## 2. The Circumstances of Termination
1. **Date & Time:** [Date of Termination], between approximately 10:00 AM and 11:00 AM.
2. **Method of Summons:** The employer sent me a message via WhatsApp asking if I could come in to "discuss rosters." There was no indication of a disciplinary or termination meeting.
3. **Physical Setting:** 
   - Upon arrival, she escorted me outside to the back storage area of the Town and Country Tavern where chairs are placed near the chemical storage.
   - A commercial security camera is situated directly covering this area (which I believe captures footage and potentially audio).
4. **The Interaction (Approx. 5 Minutes):**
   - Without prior warning, written notice, or an opportunity to bring a support person, the employer abruptly terminated my employment.
   - She raised a sudden, unsubstantiated, and false allegation regarding "stealing money" or missing cash.
   - In total shock and disbelief at the ambush, I said "sorry" purely out of stunned shock and walked off.
   - During the exchange, she made the explicit, spiteful threat:
     > **"You will never work in this town again."**
   - The entire encounter lasted less than five minutes.

---

## 3. Immediate Aftermath & First-Complaint Corroboration
1. Immediately upon leaving the premises, in a state of acute emotional distress and shock, I drove directly home to 19 Bowser Court, Wangaratta.
2. I immediately disclosed the full incident, the false allegation of theft, and her specific threat to my partner, **Zinta Cook**.
3. I was crying and visibly distraught; Zinta consoled me and can corroborate my immediate distress and contemporaneous disclosure of the employer's words.

---

## 4. Current Outstanding Matters & Damages
1. **Unpaid Final Wages:** An amount of **$920.00** remains unpaid and unlawfully withheld by the employer following termination.
2. **Defamation & Malicious Blackballing:**
   - The employer belongs to a prominent, wealthy, long-established local family.
   - Since the termination, she has actively published false and defamatory allegations regarding my honesty and character to third parties and business contacts in Wangaratta, fulfilling her threat that I "would never work in this town again" and causing severe reputational and career damage in a small regional community.
