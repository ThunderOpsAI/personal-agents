# Hospital Cover Comparison: Joint Replacement & Pain Interventions

*Prices are approximate monthly premiums for a single adult (before age-based rebates or loadings) with a standard $500 or $750 excess.*

### The Golden Rule for Frequent Operations
If you are having operations every 6-12 months, **you must ensure the policy caps your Excess at "Once per Calendar Year"**. All policies recommended below charge the excess on your first hospital admission, but your second admission in the same year will have a $0 excess.

### Strategy for Downgrading

To escape the massive Medibank Gold premium, you have two safe paths:

**Path 1: The "Budget Gold" Route (Frank or AHM)**
*   **Why it works:** You keep a true "Gold" policy, so there is absolutely zero risk of getting caught out by a missing clinical category when you go in for a complex pain intervention or joint replacement.
*   **The Catch:** You use a budget brand, meaning you manage everything via an app rather than walking into a branch.

**Path 2: The "Silver Plus Assure / Security" Route (Bupa or Medibank)**
*   **Why it works:** Under Australian law, Joint Replacements and Pain Management with Device are strictly Gold categories. However, funds are allowed to take a Silver policy and manually add Gold categories to it to create a "Silver Plus" policy. 
*   **The Catch:** You must triple-check the specific Product Disclosure Statement (PDS) before you sign. You must see both **"Joint Replacements"** AND **"Pain Management with Device"** listed under the *Included* column. If the policy just says "Silver Plus," it might have added Cataracts instead of Joint Replacements. 

**Recommendation:** Drop down to **AHM Gold** or **Frank Gold**. It saves you roughly $50–$70 a month (up to $800 a year) while guaranteeing every single surgery, joint replacement, and neurostimulator implant is covered unconditionally.
