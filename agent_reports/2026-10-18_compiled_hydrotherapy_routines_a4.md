> *This documentation provides clinical decision support and rehabilitation protocols synthesized for personal reference and guided physical therapy. It does not constitute independent medical diagnosis or prescription. All exercises must be cleared by your treating neurosurgeon, orthopedic surgeon, and physiotherapist. If sharp pain, radicular symptoms, dizziness, or chest tightness occur, stop immediately, safely exit the pool, and seek prompt clinician evaluation.*

## Detailed Protocols by Category

### Category 1: Foundational Hydrotherapy Pool Routines (`h1`–`h4`)

#### Routine `h1`: Buoyancy Spinal Decompression (25 min)
* **Objective:** Leverage warm water (32°C–34°C) buoyancy to offload up to 90% of gravitational compression across lumbar discs and cervical fusion hardware.
* **Steps:**
  1. **Buoyant Vertical Traction (6 min 15s):** Place noodle under axillae in deep water where feet do not touch the floor. Let legs, pelvis, and lumbar spine hang completely weightless. Breathe deeply into lower abdomen.
  2. **Gentle Water Knee-to-Chest (6 min 15s):** While suspended by noodle or floating belt, slowly draw both knees toward chest. Hold for 3 seconds, feeling gentle opening across posterior facet joints. Release slowly. 3 sets of 10 reps.
  3. **Aquatic Torso Pendulum (6 min 15s):** In vertical suspension, allow lower legs to gently sway side to side in a smooth arc. Cervical spine remains strictly still and eyes forward.
  4. **Supine Water Float with Head Support (6 min 15s):** Recline onto floating head pillow and noodle under knees. Close eyes, slow heart rate, deep parasympathetic relaxation.

#### Routine `h2`: Aquatic Multi-Planar Gait & Walking (20 min)
* **Objective:** Retrain symmetrical gait, weight transfer, and pelvic leveling in chest-deep water without impact forces.
* **Steps:**
  1. **Forward Water Marching (5 min):** Chest-deep water, walk forward driving knees up to 70° with conscious heel-strike and toe push-off. Maintain upright posture with gaze forward.
  2. **Backward Heel-to-Toe Walking (5 min):** Step backward toe-to-heel, engaging hamstrings and glutes while resisting water pressure.
  3. **Lateral Sidestepping (5 min):** Face pool side, step laterally across lane with continuous tension against water drag. Strengthens gluteus medius and stabilizes SI joint.
  4. **Warm Water Calming Rest (5 min):** Rest standing against pool wall, hands on rail, taking slow diaphragmatic breaths.

#### Routine `h3`: Aquatic Hip & Core Stabilization (20 min)
* **Objective:** Build pelvic girdle stability, hip abductor strength, and deep core bracing using fluid drag and flotation resistance.
* **Steps:**
  1. **Aquatic Standing Hip Abduction (5 min):** Stand holding pool wall. Keep body tall and sweep right leg laterally out against water resistance. 3 sets x 12 reps each leg.
  2. **Kickboard Core Press-Down (5 min):** Hold kickboard flat with both hands at chest level. Brace core, engage transversus abdominis, and push kickboard straight down into water column. 3 sets x 10 reps.
  3. **Aquatic Bicycle Pedaling (5 min):** Rest upper body back across noodle, pedal legs smoothly through water column. Maintain steady cadence without knee pain.
  4. **Floating Spine Rest (5 min):** Supine horizontal float with limb relaxation.

#### Routine `h4`: Warm Water Relaxation & Lymphatic Flush (20 min)
* **Objective:** Hydrostatic pressure gradient (increasing with depth) drives venous and lymphatic return, reducing soft-tissue edema and calming central pain sensitisation.
* **Steps:**
  1. **Deep Water Submersion Breathing (5 min):** Chest-deep water submersion. Feel water pressure assist deep, full exhalations to reset autonomic tone.
  2. **Aquatic Arm Sweeps (5 min):** Submerged circular sweeping patterns with hands like wiping water. Keep elbows soft and hands strictly below shoulder height.
  3. **Ankle & Foot Water Mobility (5 min):** Full circular rotations, point, and flex motions at pool step to pump venous calf muscle pump.
  4. **Supported Supine Water Float (5 min):** Total passive surrender in warm pool with head and knee float supports.

### Category 2: Rapid & Express Aqua Resets (`A1`–`A6`)

* **A1: Water Reset (6 min):** 2 min forward/backward walking, 1 min high knee lifts, 1 min gentle torso sway, 2 min supported recovery.
* **A2: Side-Step Reset (6 min):** 2 min lateral sidestepping, 1 min rail knee lifts, 1 min hip abductions, 2 min pool edge relaxation.
* **A3: Balanced Aqua (14 min):** 3 min water walking, 2 min supported straight kicks, 2 min rail hip abductions, 2 min low arm push/pull, 2 min knee lifts, 3 min floating recovery.
* **A4: Low-Impact Aqua (14 min):** 3 min forward/back walking, 2 min lateral strides, 2 min supported pendular leg swings, 2 min low chest water presses, 2 min knee drives, 3 min warm rest.
* **A5: Full Aqua Session (25 min):** 5 min progressive walking, 3 min suspended cycling, 3 min wall-supported flutter kicks, 3 min standing lateral leg raises, 3 min water sculling push/pull, 3 min easy stride, 5 min passive noodle float.
* **A6: Aqua Variety (25 min):** 5 min multi-speed walking, 3 min side strides, 3 min rail knee lifts, 3 min aquatic leg kicks, 3 min arm resistance sculling, 3 min walking cooldown, 5 min recovery float.

### Category 3: Medium-Intensity Band-Resisted Protocols (30–45 min)

#### Protocol `H-MED1`: 30-Minute Dynamic Aqua Balance & Banded Core
* **Equipment:** Medium Theraband (Green/Blue), Pool Rail, Flotation Noodle.
* **Structure:**
  1. **Dynamic Aquatic Warm-Up (5 min):** Forward heel-to-toe stride (2m), backward stride (2m), lateral side-stepping with rail touch (1m).
  2. **Banded Rail-Anchored Hip Abduction & Extension (8 min):** Anchor medium band to rail, loop around ankle. 3 sets x 12 reps lateral sweeps + 3 sets x 12 reps glute extension kicks per leg.
  3. **Pool Wall Pelvic Neutral & Core Wall Press (7 min):** Back flat to wall, engage core and flatten lumbar curve (10s hold x 6 reps). Submerge noodle at chest and press downward (3 sets x 10 reps).
  4. **Noodle-Supported Deep Water Core Suspensions (6 min):** Noodle under armpits in chest/deep water. Alternate slow, controlled knee lifts to chest against water drag (3 sets x 15 reps).
  5. **Buoyancy Decompression Cool-Down (4 min):** Supported supine float with deep diaphragmatic breathing.

#### Protocol `H-MED2`: 45-Minute Total Body Aquatic Conditioning & Scapular Rowing
* **Equipment:** Heavy/Medium Theraband, Pool Rail, Kickboard, Water Dumbbells.
* **Structure:**
  1. **Hydrodynamic Gait & Scapular Activation (10 min):** Multi-directional strides (5m), submerged arm sweeps with chin retractions (5m).
  2. **Rail-Anchored Submerged Band Rows & W-Rows (10 min):** Heavy band anchored at waist height to rail. Low row squeezing shoulder blades (3 sets x 12). W-row focusing on lower trapezius with elbows low (3 sets x 10).
  3. **Banded Pool-Rail Shallow Squat Presses (10 min):** Heavy band looped to rail and pelvis. Facing rail, perform shallow squat (max 45° knee flexion), driving forcefully through heels and glutes (4 sets x 10 reps).
  4. **Kickboard Aquatic Core Presses & Twists (10 min):** Submerge kickboard vertically into water column (3 sets x 12). Gentle torso turns with board submerged (neck rigid, eyes forward) (3 sets x 10).
  5. **Decompression & Ankle Flushes (5 min):** Ankle rotations, calf stretch against pool step, easy breathing.

### Category 4: Advanced Heavy Resistance & Athlete Protocols (60–90 min)

#### Protocol `H-EXP1`: 60-Minute Heavy Hydro Resistance & Power Protocol
* **Equipment:** Heavy Theraband (Black/Silver), Heavy Loop Bands, Pool Rail, Water Dumbbells, Flotation Belt.
* **Structure:**
  1. **Dynamic Thermal & Gait Warm-Up (10 min):** High-knee marching (3m), lateral walk-outs (3m), submerged pendulum sweeps (4m).
  2. **Heavy Banded Pool-Rail Power Squat Pulses (10 min):** Heavy band anchored to rail and pelvis. Shallow explosive pulses (30°–45°), driving through glutes against band tension and water drag. 4 sets x 12 reps (60s rest).
  3. **Submerged Unilateral Band Rows & Chest Press (10 min):** Anchor band to rail. Left arm: heavy rows (4x12). Right arm: controlled micro-range rows below 80° shoulder elevation (4x8).
  4. **Banded Submerged Knee Extensions & Lateral Kicks (12 min):** Ankle band to rail. Facing away: knee extensions (4x12/leg). Facing side: hip abductions against band (4x12/leg).
  5. **Deep Water Suspended Core Crucible (10 min):** Flotation belt on, deep water. Alternate 45s high-cadence bicycle pedaling with 45s double knee-to-chest tucks. 5 cycles.
  6. **Thermal Decompression & Fascial Stretching (8 min):** Buoyancy-assisted hamstring stretches, calf release, passive noodle traction.

#### Protocol `H-EXP2`: 75-Minute Multi-Planar Hydro Endurance & Kinetic Chain Overhaul
* **Equipment:** Heavy Loop Bands, Heavy Therabands, Pool Rail, Water Dumbbells, Pool Noodle, Belt.
* **Structure:**
  1. **Hydrodynamic Activation Circuit (12 min):** 400m water stride combination (forward, backward, carioca crossover).
  2. **Banded Lateral Walk-Outs & Monster Walks (15 min):** Heavy loop band around ankles in chest-deep water. Lateral walk across pool (4x15 steps L/R). Monster zig-zag walks (4x12 strides).
  3. **Submerged Heavy Dumbbell Upper Body Matrix (13 min):** Water dumbbells submerged at mid-chest depth. Bicep curls into low-punches (3x12). Torso rotations with dumbbells at ribs (3x12).
  4. **Rail-Supported Explosive Glute Kickbacks (12 min):** Band anchored to rail, looped to ankle. Drive straight back into maximum glute contraction with 2s peak hold. 4 sets x 12 reps per leg.
  5. **Deep Water Interval Treading & Core Sprints (13 min):** Belt on, deep water. 1 min max effort treading / churn + 1 min slow recovery sculling. 6 rounds.
  6. **Buoyancy Elongation & Lymphatic Drainage (10 min):** Full supine float with noodles under head and knees. Passive hydrostatic lymphatic clearance.

#### Protocol `H-EXP3`: 90-Minute Master Hydro Athlete Rehabilitation Protocol
* **Equipment:** Full Hydro Resistance Kit (Heavy bands, Hydro paddles, Flotation belt, Noodles, Rail).
* **Structure:**
  1. **Phase 1: Hydrodynamic Multi-Speed Gait & Thermal Prep (15 min):** Progressive water walking (slow -> moderate -> fast), submerged shoulder sculling and scapular retractions.
  2. **Phase 2: Heavy Band Upper-Body Kinetic Chain (20 min):** Low rows (3x12), W-rows (3x10), shoulder internal/external rotation complexes (3x10/arm, right arm low), scapular protraction/retraction holds (3x12).
  3. **Phase 3: Lower Kinetic Chain & Pelvic Power (20 min):** Banded squats with 3-second isometric pause at 45° (4x10), banded knee extensions for quad hypertrophy (4x12/leg), banded lateral walks & glute kickbacks (4x12/leg).
  4. **Phase 4: Deep Water Aquatic Core Crucible (18 min):** Suspended scissor kicks (4x45s), submerged kickboard presses (4x12), deep water high-knee sprint intervals (5 rounds of 1 min on / 1 min off).
  5. **Phase 5: Neuromuscular Gait Integration & Balance (10 min):** Single-leg balance holds in chest-deep water (2 min/leg), heel-to-toe tightrope water walking with contralateral arm sweeps (6 min).
  6. **Phase 6: Passive Decompression & Thermal Surrender (7 min):** Noodle-supported vertical spinal traction and supine float with diaphragmatic parasympathetic breathwork.
